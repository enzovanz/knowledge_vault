## Overview

_Goodbye, Things_ combines personal experience with practical reflections on minimalism. Fumio Sasaki examines how possessions can affect attention, identity, habits, and well-being, then describes ways to reduce unnecessary belongings and become more deliberate about what remains.

## Goal

Understand the psychological and practical motivations behind minimalism. Identify ideas that can improve my relationship with possessions, reduce unnecessary clutter, and create more space for activities and relationships that matter.
