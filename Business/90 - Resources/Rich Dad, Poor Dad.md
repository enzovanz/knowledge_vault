## Overview

_Rich Dad, Poor Dad_ contrasts two attitudes toward work, money, and wealth to introduce basic financial-literacy ideas. It focuses on the distinction between assets and liabilities, the importance of cash flow, acquiring income-producing assets, developing financial skills, and thinking beyond employment income alone.

## Goal

Build a simple mental model for examining personal cash flow, assets, liabilities, and sources of income. Use its ideas to question conventional assumptions about work and money while evaluating its anecdotal claims critically before applying them.
