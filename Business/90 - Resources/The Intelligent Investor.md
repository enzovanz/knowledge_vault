## Overview

_The Intelligent Investor_ presents Benjamin Graham's approach to value investing: treating stocks as ownership in businesses, estimating value independently of market price, and buying with a margin of safety. It distinguishes investing from speculation and emphasizes diversification, disciplined portfolio policy, long-term thinking, and emotional control when markets fluctuate.

## Goal

Learn a durable framework for evaluating investments without reacting to market sentiment. Understand intrinsic value, margin of safety, the difference between defensive and enterprising investing, and how temperament influences long-term results.
