## Overview

_The Richest Man in Babylon_ uses short parables set in ancient Babylon to present practical principles for managing personal finances. Its recurring lessons include saving part of every income, controlling expenses, investing carefully, protecting capital from avoidable loss, and steadily improving one's ability to earn.

## Goal

Extract a small set of memorable rules for saving, budgeting, investing, and managing financial risk. Evaluate how those principles apply to modern personal finance and turn the useful ones into repeatable habits.
