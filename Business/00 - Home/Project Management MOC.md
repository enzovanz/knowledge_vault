## Overview

Project management studies how temporary initiatives are defined, planned, executed, monitored, and completed to produce an intended outcome within constraints such as scope, time, cost, quality, and risk.

## Areas

- Goals, scope, and requirements
- Planning, estimation, and scheduling
- Budget and resource management
- Risk and quality management
- Stakeholder communication
- Team coordination and leadership
- Predictive, agile, and hybrid approaches
- Monitoring, delivery, and project closure
