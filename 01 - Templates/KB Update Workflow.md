## Capture

Create a rough concept in `99 - Concept Inbox` or save an unprocessed source in `100 - Resource Inbox`. A title and a few words are enough.

## Triage

Move concepts you want to study later into the appropriate domain folder under `98 - Concept Backlog`. Keep only concepts you plan to process soon in `99 - Concept Inbox`.

## Process

1. Decide whether it is a concept or resource and insert the matching template.
2. Explain it in your own words and add one concrete example.
3. Link a source or related concept only when the connection is useful.
4. Add the finished concept to one relevant MOC.
5. Move a finished concept to the relevant domain's `10 - Knowledge` folder or a processed resource to its `90 - Resources` folder.

A note is done when you can understand it without reopening the original source. Do not add properties, tags, or links just to fill fields.
