## Overview

_The Pragmatic Programmer_ is a software engineering book focused on the practices, habits, and mindset involved in becoming a more effective programmer. It covers topics ranging from personal responsibility and continuous learning to software design, debugging, testing, automation, and maintaining adaptable systems.

## Goal

To develop stronger software engineering habits and understand practical principles that can improve how I design, write, debug, maintain, and evolve software.
