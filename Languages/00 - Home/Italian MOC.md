## Overview

Italian study develops listening, reading, speaking, and writing skills through grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, and practice. This map connects Italian learning topics and resources.

## Learning path

### Foundations

- [ ] Build everyday Italian vocabulary and pronunciation.
- [ ] Practise basic grammar and sentence patterns (A1–A2).

### Core topics

- [ ] Consolidate intermediate grammar (B1–B2).
- [ ] Combine reading and listening with regular speaking and writing practice.
